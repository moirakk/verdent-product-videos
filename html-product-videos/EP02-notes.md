# EP02: Create App

Visual baseline: Manager v009. First revision: EP02/v001. Runtime: 32 seconds.

## Story

- 0-3s: Dimensional application reveal.
- 3-6.5s: "Build a booking app for my studio."
- 6.5-10s: Calendar, availability, and booking modules separate in 3D.
- 10-17s: Components assemble into one application.
- 17-22.5s: Preview selects September 18 and 10:30.
- 22.5-26s: Booking action produces a confirmation.
- 26-29s: Hold on the completed booking flow.
- 29-32s: Supplied Verdent logo and closing line.

The booking application is an illustrative animation, not a deployed service or a recording of Verdent output. No actual reservation is made. The film does not claim publication, security checks, production readiness, or a real booking backend.
