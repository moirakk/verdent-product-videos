# Verdent product animation series

## Approved visual baseline

The user selected Manager v009 as the visual reference for subsequent episodes.
Reference: `versions/v009/EP01-manager.html`. Preserve this snapshot unchanged.

- Portrait 9:16, autoplay, at most 35 seconds; Manager runs 32 seconds.
- White space, dark green and brand green, dimensional product components.
- Real Three.js geometry for hero objects: thickness, bevels, material highlights, perspective and coordinated movement.
- Short, bold two-line headlines with staged word reveals and an emphasized green second line. No permanent explanatory paragraph at the top.
- Product-ad pacing: anticipation, movement, settling, then enough time to read.
- Carry objects across transitions; unfold, assemble or merge them rather than repeatedly fade between slides.
- Content should visibly change state: select, save, fill, connect, move, review.
- Keep copy clear of moving objects. Test intermediate frames as well as settled frames at desktop and mobile sizes.
- Use the supplied Verdent logo for the calm closing frame. Do not invent or redraw it.
- Do not reintroduce the decorative circular pedestal or flat hub-and-spoke integration diagram.
