# Verdent Feature Alignment

Purpose: align every product video with Verdent's actual product meaning before writing final Seedance prompts.

Important production rule:
Every episode ends with a clean logo frame. The AI video prompt should leave a clear logo-safe area, but the real Verdent logo should be added in post-production from the uploaded official logo file. Do not ask Seedance to invent or redraw the logo.

## Official Product Backbone

Verdent Manager is positioned as an AI technical co-founder: the user describes an outcome in plain language, and Verdent plans the work, executes with project context, remembers preferences, and returns product progress.

The official Manager flow is:

1. Describe the outcome.
2. Verdent plans the work.
3. Workers run in parallel.
4. Review and iterate.

Manager also includes task decomposition, parallel execution, remote triggers, result delivery, contextual replies, messaging triggers, and long-term memory.

Verdent Cloud is the browser-based flow for building, previewing, publishing, sharing, and iterating web apps. Its app creation flow supports templates and Design Mode. Design Mode is for choosing the product's visual direction before building a working app.

Data analysis, document processing, and research reports are surfaced as direct interface entries. Treat them as knowledge-work outcomes, not as generic dashboards.

## Series Principle

Do not make every episode look like a Kanban board.

Kanban / phases / workers belong mainly to EP1 Manager. Other episodes should show the specific artifact being created or analyzed:

- App preview
- Design direction
- Slide deck
- Analysis result
- Document summary
- Research report
- Image asset
- Video preview
